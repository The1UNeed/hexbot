A bot used to have no idea which of your other bots could help it. If it did send another bot a message, the exchange landed as a stray conversation in the other bot's list.

As of today's nightly, your bots work as a team.

## How it works

Every bot reads a short description of your other bots. If you've written a description for a bot, that's what its teammates read. If you haven't, Hexbot writes one from the bot's name, title, and soul. You never see it in the app, and it's rewritten when the soul changes.

When Juniper is writing a newsletter and needs September's numbers, it can ask Ledger directly. The two talk in a private conversation of their own. A bot that's asked helps the way it would help you, and a request from another bot never overrides yours.

## Watching it happen

Under Juniper's reply, the two faces turn toward each other with a line that reads "Juniper is asking Ledger". Ledger's answer appears under that line. When it's done, you can open the conversation and read the whole exchange.

Both bots learn from it, too. Dreaming reads these conversations along with the rest.

To keep a bot to itself, turn off **Message other bots** in its Tools settings.

## Also in this nightly: a calmer chat

The chat now works like a texting app. Replies no longer type themselves out and then jump. Each message a bot finishes arrives whole, as its own bubble. While a bot works, one plain line sits beside its face, such as "Scout is running a command", and you can click it to see the steps. When the turn ends, a quiet "Worked for 46s" takes its place.
