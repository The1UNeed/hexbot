Until today, every bot was equal, and everything around them was up to you: making bots, putting them in rooms, scheduling their jobs, changing their settings. Starting with today's nightly, one bot does that for you.

## Your primary bot

Setup now ends in a chat with **HexBot**, your primary bot. It introduces itself and offers to make the bots you need. Tell it "I need someone to check my newsletter before it goes out", and it makes a writer for the job, with a face, a soul, and a working folder.

![Maya asks HexBot for someone to check her newsletter, and HexBot makes Quill, a newsletter editor that appears in the bot list.](/screens/hexbot.webp)

From its own chat, HexBot can:

- make, change, and delete bots;
- make rooms, change who is in them, and choose the main bot;
- schedule jobs for any bot;
- change settings, skills, and which connectors a bot uses.

## It always asks first

HexBot asks before it deletes a bot, room, or job, changes an approval mode or a working folder, or changes a setting. That holds even in Bypass. It never sees your API keys or connector credentials, and it doesn't touch network, service, or billing settings.

These powers live only in HexBot's own chat. In rooms, scheduled jobs, and conversations with other bots, it's an ordinary bot.

## One, and it stays

You can rename HexBot and give it a different face, but you can't delete it. No other bot can take the handle `hexbot`.

If you used Hexbot before today, the bot list offers to add HexBot, or to make one of your existing bots primary instead. [The primary bot](/docs/bots-and-sections/#the-primary-bot) has the details.
