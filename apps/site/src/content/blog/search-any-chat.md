A chat that never ends is easy to keep and hard to search. As of today's nightly, you can find anything in it.

## Search

Open the side panel beside a chat and choose **Search**. Results come from the whole chat, grouped as messages, files, visuals, and soul and memory. Every word you type has to match, and case and accents don't matter. Choose a message or file and the chat scrolls to it and highlights it, even if it's months back. A visual opens beside the chat. Rooms have a Search button beside Room settings.

On the phone, the search button is in the chat's header.

## Fingerprint

A new **Fingerprint** tab in the side panel shows two cards: the bot's **Soul** and its **Memory**, with the day each last changed. Open one for a full-window editor with bold, italic, headings, and lists. It saves as you type. Your phone shows the same two cards on the bot's profile.

The **Library** tab also lists the visuals a bot made, newest first.

## The sidebar shows where you left off

Each bot in the sidebar now shows the latest message in its chat, yours or the bot's, the way a messaging app does.

## Also in this nightly

- **Messages that didn't send.** Your bubble greys out while it sends. If it doesn't go through, it shows **Not sent · Retry** right under it, and Retry never leaves two copies.
- **Plugins in three tabs.** Settings, Plugins now has tabs for Connectors, MCP servers, and Skills, and every row has an icon.
- **Code and browsing on the bots' computer only.** Bots now run code and browse only on their own computer, never on the daemon's. Every bot has it, and its controls moved to Settings, Manuals, Computer use. Passwords are typed only there too.
